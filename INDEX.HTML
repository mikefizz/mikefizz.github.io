About MIKEFIZZ

Michael Adah, professionally known as MIKEFIZZ, is an independent Afrobeats and Afro Hip-Hop artist from Odelle Village, Ekwu-Ekenobi Community, Ekeh Ward, Okpokwu Local Government Area, Benue State, Nigeria. Born on 02 February 1996, he began his music career in 2013.
